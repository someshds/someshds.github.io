About Sally Oasis

Sally Oasis Paintings

bright joyful art

I’ve painted all my life and absolutely love it!

Sally Scott BA Hons PGCE aka Sally Oasis

It all began in 1996 on a wet and windy market stall in Crawley, West Sussex which later on with backing from The Prince’s Trust, enabled me to establish my first high street gallery in Horley, Surrey selling my paintings and developing a picture framing business. I later went on to teach art along with television presenting for the Painting and Drawing Channel.

In December 2023 we refurbished the old dairy farm buildings on the beautiful William Robinson Gravetye Estate, West Hoathly, Sussex.

Here’s a link to a video showing us refurbishing the building.

From walks into the studio

Passionately inspired by flowers, trees and the countryside, I absorb views, shapes, patterns and sounds on my walks, then back in my studio reinterpret these observations and experiences onto canvas for you to enjoy, connect with and make your own.

I take commissions so if there’s something special you’d like, do ask.

You’re more than welcome to come and visit the studio and gallery – I’m there most days but doublecheck with me first just in case if you’re making a special trip.

Prints, canvases and stockists

I now also produce the most beautiful, luxurious giclée limited edition prints and printed canvases in a variety of sizes, framed or flat wrapped and posted directly to you. Complimentary U.K. postage for limited edition giclée prints and printed canvases.

I’m proud to supply a selection of my prints to Royal Botanic Gardens Kew London and Wakehurst Place West Sussex.

I also supply my cards and prints to Coolings Garden Centre at Wych Cross and local farm shops.
