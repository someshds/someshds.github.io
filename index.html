About Sally Oasis

Sally Scott BA Hons PGCE

aka Sally Oasis

It all began in 1996 on a wet and windy market stall in Crawley, West Sussex which later on with backing from The Prince’s Trust, enabled me to establish my first high street gallery in Horley, Surrey selling my paintings and developing a picture framing business. I later went on to teach art along with television presenting for the Painting and Drawing Channel.

In December 2023 we refurbished the old dairy farm buildings on the beautiful William Robinson Gravetye Estate, West Hoathly, Sussex.
